# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 2.0.0-beta.2

- The admin menu's group headings, the health bar under it and the phone's bottom bar use the theme's heading font.
- **Status page**: a new switch puts the default theme on Simplify's public status page, in light and dark as the
  visitor's device prefers. Off by default; it shows with a Simplify that has the status page hook.

## 2.0.0-beta.1

- First version on the Hub: your own themes for the panel, with colours for light and dark, a background, fonts (bundled or
  uploaded), shapes and console colours with Simplify, a live preview, ready-made themes, export and import, and
  **My theme** for everyone when **Let people pick** is on.
- Moving background effects (shooting stars, snow, fireflies, rain, aurora, bubbles) with amount, speed, size,
  strength, glow, direction and colour.
- A gradient editor: 2 to 6 colours with blend points, linear, radial or conic, angle, centre and a seamless repeat,
  one gradient for light and one for dark.
- Over a gradient or picture, Simplify's admin pages sit on one card with a matching title bar; the editor's tabs and
  preview stay in sight while it scrolls.
- Light and dark each have their own background (plain, gradient or picture). The title bar says when the theme you
  edit is not the one people see, with Make default; pictures and fonts a theme uses are marked In use.
- The ⋯ menu shows its words, Repeat off puts the colours back, and theme tiles follow light or dark.
